★ Questions That Will Wow Them ▼
Ask exactly 2. Q1-Q2 are primary. Q3-Q6 are backups. Each block shows exactly who to ask it to and why it lands.
02 Keyword Map: JD Requirements to Your Best Answers ▼
03 Core Stories: C1–C6 ▼
5-6 anchor stories. Every question maps back to one of these. Labeled C1-C6 for precise cross-referencing. Know them cold.
04 Fallback Stories: F1–F11 ▼
10-12 supporting stories. Labeled F1-F11. Selected from the standard pool based on JD relevance. Shorter STAR. Two tip badges each. Linked from core stories and Q&A fallback bars.
05 Role-Specific Questions ▼
Derived from the JD. Every responsibility and requirement gets a prepared answer. Always include: why leaving, why this role, clearance, location/relocation.
06 Behavioral Questions ▼
Always-asked questions calibrated to this role. Full STAR. All have fallback bars. Order: Strength / Weakness / Competing Priorities / Problem No One Caught / Ambiguous Environment / Influence Without Authority / Pushback Under Pressure.
