Open-ended question: close the gap before answering Every card
Ask, frame, outline, stop. Clarify first, then hand it back.
▼
Use when: any design, hypothetical, theoretical, or broad behavioral question with no clear scope.
Direct answer

1. Ask first. 2 to 4 clarifying questions: users, primary goal, constraints, what success looks like. For a broad behavioral question, one scope question: “Do you want a specific example or my general approach?”

2. Frame in one sentence. Restate the problem and the worst outcome before solving it.

3. Outline only. 3 or 4 points, one line each, 60 to 90 seconds.

4. Stop and hand it back. “Where do you want me to go deeper?”

5. Keep detail in reserve. Their follow-ups show what they care about.

Sample clarifying questions for [COMPANY]'s domain
  • “[Who are the users or operators of this system?]”
  • “[What is the primary goal, and what does success look like?]”
  • “[What constraints do I have: safety, cost, schedule, regulatory?]”
  • “[What is the worst failure outcome we need to design against?]”
Answer length guide

Behavioral STAR: 60 to 90 seconds. Design outline: 90 seconds after clarifying. Theoretical: 30 to 45 seconds (answer, one reason, one example, stop).
