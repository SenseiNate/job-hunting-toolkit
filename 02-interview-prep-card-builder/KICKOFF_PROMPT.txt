AFTER AN INTERVIEW ROUND HAPPENS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

First, in plain language, tell Claude:
  - What was actually asked (as close to verbatim as you can recall)
  - What landed well and what didn't
  - Any new intel: interviewer names/titles, process details, timeline,
    next-round format
  - Anything unusual about the round format (case study, live coding,
    whiteboard system design, work-sample walkthrough) that might call for a
    round-specific extra — see README.md's Chat-Specific Extras section.
    Extras built for one round apply only to that round's card.

Then send exactly this, filled in:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Update the card for Round [N] with [interviewer name(s)].
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Claude will re-audit per README's Debrief and Re-Encode Loop and rebuild the Story Sheet if
Resume Stories, JD Question Stories, or Extra Stories content changed.
